Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lingo50 – Learn Languages ("Lingo50", "we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use the Lingo50 mobile application.

We designed Lingo50 as a language-learning application that helps users practice vocabulary, sentences, reading, listening, pronunciation, quizzes, and language-learning progress.

1. Information We Collect

Lingo50 is designed to minimize the amount of personal information that it collects.

1.1 Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store learning-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Selected native and learning languages
  • Lesson progress
  • Completed lessons
  • Favorites
  • Revision information
  • Learning streaks
  • XP and achievement progress
  • Daily learning preferences
  • App settings such as theme preferences

This information is used to provide the learning experience and maintain your progress.

1.2 Speech Recognition and Microphone

Some Lingo50 features may use speech recognition to allow you to practice pronunciation or speaking.

When you use a speaking feature, the App may request microphone permission. Audio captured for speech recognition is processed through the speech-recognition service available on your device or operating system.

Lingo50 does not intentionally use the microphone in the background. Microphone access is requested only when you actively use a speech-related feature.

1.3 Text-to-Speech

Lingo50 can use your device's text-to-speech service to pronounce learning content. Text is sent to the operating-system speech service only when you request audio pronunciation.

The availability and processing of speech services may depend on your device manufacturer, operating system, installed language packs, and third-party speech providers.

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Lingo50 does not require you to create an account to use the basic learning features.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, payment card numbers, contact lists, or precise location information through the basic learning functionality of the App.

3. How We Use Information

Information available to the App is used to:

  • Save and display your learning progress
  • Remember your selected languages
  • Provide lessons and revision activities
  • Provide vocabulary and sentence practice
  • Provide speech and pronunciation features
  • Maintain streaks, XP and achievements
  • Remember app preferences
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the App

4. Third-Party Services

Some Lingo50 features may rely on services provided by third parties, such as operating-system speech recognition and text-to-speech services.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Important: If future versions of Lingo50 add advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, AI services, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those services are introduced.

5. Advertising

The current version of Lingo50 may not display advertising.

Future versions may introduce advertising, including optional rewarded advertisements used to unlock additional learning activities. If advertising services are introduced, the relevant third-party advertising providers and data practices will be disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

Learning information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the App, removed through available app settings, or you uninstall the App or clear its application data.

Because local data is stored on your device, uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may remove your saved learning progress.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the design of the App to minimize unnecessary collection and exposure of user information.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Lingo50 is an educational language-learning application. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information to us may contact us using the contact information below.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the features available in your version of Lingo50, you may be able to:

  • Change your selected languages
  • Manage learning preferences
  • Remove locally stored app data by clearing app storage
  • Uninstall the application
  • Control microphone permissions through Android settings
  • Control notification permissions through device settings

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
